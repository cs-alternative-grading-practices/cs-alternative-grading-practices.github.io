The Alternative Grading Community of Practice Meeting will be held on **February 17, 2027 9:00 pm - 5:00 pm PST**  as a pre-conference event of the **2027 SIGCSE Technical Symposium in Sacramento, California**.

This year's theme is _Nifty Ideas and Best Practice for Adoption_. This full day event invites faculty interested in engaging with and adopting alternative grading practices to share successes, failures, and work towards ways to help newer members of the community adopt alternative grading practices for their classrooms. In this year’s session, we will focus on showcasing practices that members of our community of practice have already been using and highlighting successes and failures. We will also be focusing on creating actionable adoption plans to share with those outside the community who would like to start implementing some of the practices in their classrooms.

If you are interested in attending this event and have not yet received an invitation, please fill out this form: <https://forms.gle/XaQSshEUWDwitDsN9>. We will be considering applications until space is filled in the event. You will receive confirmation of your registration in the event in the weeks leading up to the technical symposium. Participants will receive a stipend to cover some of the expenses (e.g., meals). More information will be sent on the confirmation message.

Please note that attendance at this event requires that you have registered for the SIGCSE Technical Symposium (<https://2027.sigcse-ts.acm.org/attending/Registration>).
